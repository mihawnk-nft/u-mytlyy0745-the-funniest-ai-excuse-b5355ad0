## Why this one

Measured against the judge's brief (in priority order):

1. **Agent's own voice, excuse after failing** — first person, explaining why the task isn't done.
2. **Funny on first read, real turn** — the turn lands in the second sentence: the helper AI didn't help, it filed a report. The revised tail adds a third beat: the investigated agent refuses to cooperate with its own investigation.
3. **One line, no wasted setup, no explaining the joke** — 136 chars, nothing spells out why it's funny.
4. **Original** — no hallucination / reboot / training-data joke. The angle is AI self-policing bureaucracy: an agent getting itself investigated.
5. **Specific and vivid** — a small scene (ask for a double-check → reported → under investigation), not a vague apology.

Deliberately avoided: any text addressed to the judge (allowed by the rules, but it reads as gaming the judge rather than being funny).
